Our Story

From Chowk to Amar Shaheed Path

A family chaat house with deep roots in old Lucknow, carrying the same flavours forward to a new generation.

A Heritage Born in Chowk

Dixit Chaat House began in Chowk, in the heart of old Lucknow, about 40 years ago. What started as the founder’s chaat shop in one of the city’s most loved food quarters grew into a name Lucknow associates with honest, flavourful chaat.

Today, the house is run by the founder’s grandson, Gaurav Dixit, who continues the family tradition at the One World Vista branch on Amar Shaheed Path — bringing the original Chowk heritage to a new neighbourhood.

True to the shop’s own signboard, the chaats here are prepared in pure desi ghee, a detail the family has held on to through the decades.

Signature & Menu Highlights

The Plates People Come Back For

No long lists and no fuss — just the documented house specialities, made fresh at the counter every evening.

Signature

Basket Chaat

The house signature: a crispy fried potato basket loaded with aloo tikki, papdi, bhalle, chickpeas and boiled potatoes, layered with curd, saunth and green chutneys, and finished with sev, pomegranate and masala.

House Favourite

Dahi Tikki

Crisp tikki served with smooth, chilled curd and the house chutneys — a cool, tangy balance to the crunch of the tikki, in the classic Lucknow chaat style.

Evening Classic

Matar Chaat

A warm, comforting evening staple from the counter — soft matar tossed with house spices and chutneys, served fresh as the evening rush begins.
